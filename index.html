About The Slice

The slice is a unique hybrid community space conceptualized to combine an artisanal pastry bakery, a specialty coffee cafe, and a quiet, conductive study hub designed for students, remote workers, and freelancers. We offer pastries, gourmet beverages, and dedicated study amenities with high-speed internet and power outlets.
